What we teach

The Quran first, then skills for life

A child memorises the Quran step by step while also learning the deen and the skills they will need tomorrow.

Main programme

Hifdh ul Quran

Memorising the whole Quran with correct tajweed, under teachers who are huffadh themselves. Every day has three parts:

سبقSabaqThe new lesson of the day
سبقيSabqiRevision of what was learnt recently
منزلManzilRevision of everything learnt before
New Muslims

Classes for reverts

For those who have newly embraced Islam: the basics of faith, wudu and salah, and reading the Quran from the first letters, with patient teachers.

Foundations

Islamic studies

Qaida, tajweed, Arabic, salah, duas and seerah, from the very first letters upward.

Skills

Computer and other skills

Computer basics, typing and other hands-on skills, so that students can stand on their own feet.

A student's day

Timetable: Fajr to Isha

Classes start at Fajr and run until after Isha. After Isha the children go home.

TimeActivity
FajrFajr prayer in jamaa, then Sabaq (the new Hifdh lesson)
MorningSabqi and tajweed
Before DhuhrIslamic studies and Arabic
DhuhrDhuhr prayer and a break
After DhuhrComputer and skills
AsrAsr prayer, then Manzil (revision of the old)
MaghribMaghrib prayer, then recitation and revision
IshaIsha prayer in jamaa
After IshaThe children go home

The Mudir confirms the exact time of each session.
